WadaDrobe Color Accuracy Lab

A separate experiment for measuring how accurately the camera recognizes printed Sanzo Wada color samples.

Phase 1 protocol

Choose the color printed in your Wada book, keep the lighting and phone position consistent, center the printed swatch inside the target, and take repeated measurements. This tool records the camera measurement and compares it with the selected Wada standard. It does not modify the main WadaDrobe app.

What this experiment tells us

The most useful results are repeatability, the ΔE00 distance from the printed sample's intended Wada color, and whether the nearest Wada match is consistently the expected color. Because the book is a printed reproduction, treat it as a physical reference sample rather than a laboratory-grade colorimeter standard.
